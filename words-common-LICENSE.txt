web/words-common.txt: the 5,000 most frequent English content words (3-8 letters, also in words-game.txt with a part of speech),
ordered from most to least common. Used by Wordrobe Quest for Bibo's hints (Petunjuk) so hints suggest everyday words.
Derived from the word frequencies of wordfreq 3.1.1 by Robyn Speer (https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq).
